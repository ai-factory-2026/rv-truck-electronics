How to Choose the Right RV Refrigerator

1. Match Capacity to Your Needs

Weekend trips (1-2 people): 30-40 liters is usually enough Week-long trips (2-4 people): 50-60 liters Full-time living (2-4 people): 60+ liters or dual-zone

I made the mistake of buying too small at first. Watching my partner stress about "which drinks do we keep cold" got old fast.

2. Know Your Power Situation

Shore power only: Almost any 12V fridge works. Power draw matters less. Battery + inverter: Look for low watt-hour consumption. Sub-50W average is good. Solar/off-grid: This is where efficiency matters most. The ARB ZERO and Dometic CFX3 shine here.

3. Consider Dual-Zone Options

If you want frozen food AND cold drinks without a separate freezer, dual-zone is worth the extra cost. The Dometic CFX3 55IM and ARB ZERO both offer dual-zone configurations.

4. Don't Sleep on Build Quality

Your fridge will get bounced around. It'll sit in 100°F parking lots. The lid will be opened 50 times a day. Cheap plastics crack. Look for: - Reinforced corners - Quality lid seals - Solid handle mounting

Common RV Refrigerator Questions

Can I run an electric fridge off my RV battery?

Yes, but it depends on your battery capacity and power draw. A 100Ah lithium battery with 50W average draw will run a mid-size fridge for about 2 days. For longer off-grid use, add solar panels or a larger battery bank.

What's the difference between 12V portable fridges and 3-way RV fridges?

12V portable fridges use a compressor (like your home fridge) and run on 12V power. 3-way RV fridges (absorption fridges) can run on propane, 120V AC, or 12V, but they're less efficient and require leveling. Most RVers switching from 3-way to 12V notice a huge improvement in reliability.

Do portable fridges need to be level?

Compressor fridges (all the ones in this guide) can handle up to about 30° of tilt without issues. Don't stress about perfect leveling — these aren't the old absorption fridges that would stop working if you parked on a slight slope.

How long do portable fridge compressors last?

With reasonable care, expect 5-10 years of reliable service. The main killer is heat — if you can keep the condenser cool and avoid running the fridge in extreme heat, it'll last longer.
