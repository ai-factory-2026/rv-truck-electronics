About TruckGear Hub

TruckGear Hub exists for one reason: to cut through the marketing BS and give you real information about gear that actually works on the road.

My Story

Hi, I'm the person behind TruckGear Hub. I've spent years on the road — mounting cameras on rigs, wiring solar setups on vans, testing portable power stations in the middle of nowhere, and dealing with the endless stream of gear that promises everything and delivers half of it.

I started this site because I was tired of buying gear based on reviews from people who tested things for three days on their kitchen counter. I wanted to share what actually works after real miles on real roads.

What I Believe

  • Real-world testing over specs. A dash cam's resolution means nothing if it dies in summer heat.
  • Value over prestige. The most expensive option isn't always the best. Sometimes the $100 pick outperforms the $500 model.
  • Honest opinions. I don't accept payment for reviews. If something sucks, I say so.
  • Practical advice. I explain things the way I'd explain them to a buddy at the truck stop.

How I Make Money

TruckGear Hub is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. When you click my links and buy something, I earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is how I keep the lights on.

Important: My editorial decisions are never influenced by affiliate relationships. I recommend what I believe in, not what pays the most.
